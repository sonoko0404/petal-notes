Help

To add a widget, first launch Petal. Control-click your Mac desktop, choose Edit Widgets, find Petal, and add a medium or large widget. Petal cannot add a widget automatically. Edit the widget to select a card, one of five themes, a system typeface, and solid or translucent appearance.

Click a todo circle in the widget to complete it. Click card text to open the editor, or the plus button to add to that card. Option–Command–N opens Quick Capture for Inbox; change or disable this shortcut in Settings. Return saves, Shift–Return adds a line, and Escape cancels. Failed saves keep your input for retry.

Completed todos remain visible that day, then disappear from the widget while remaining in the app. Daily todos become incomplete on a new local day. Completing a daily todo before its reminder may still allow today's notification; future daily reminders continue. Sound is off by default.

Cards remain available offline. Settings shows iCloud sync state and a retry action. Turning sync off keeps the local copy. An archived or trashed card cannot be edited through its widget; restore it in Petal or edit the widget to choose another card. Trash and conflict copies are kept for 30 days.

The editor separates completed todos from incomplete rows while preserving manual order. Undo returns a todo to its previous position. Daily streaks count consecutive local days; undo removes today's credit, and a missed day starts the next streak at one. Use a row's menu or the Delete command to remove that row.

Dismissed conflict notices do not remove their copies. Settings keeps unexpired copies available for reading, copying, and recovery. Recovery creates an ordinary card and is safe to repeat. Unrecovered conflict copies stay on this Mac for 30 days and are excluded from JSON backups; recover a copy first to include it in exports.

Settings offers Windows import, JSON backup and full restore, and Markdown export. Import saves a backup first and ignores Windows window layout. Full restore replaces all synced devices. Delete All Data requires typing the product name and leaves one empty Inbox; export a backup first. If cleanup fails, retry in Settings. Cloud changes finish when iCloud is available and sync is enabled.

Help and Privacy are available offline in Settings. Feedback opens your mail app with only app version, build, macOS version, and architecture. Review the message before sending. A support mailbox will be configured before release; feedback is disabled until then.
